MailBrief

A calmer way to understand your Gmail inbox.

MailBrief summarizes Gmail message previews and helps you draft reviewable emails from your own instructions. It is a Chrome extension built to keep you in control.

What it does

  • Read-only Gmail inbox summaries and daily briefs.
  • Sender, category, and read/unread filters.
  • Draft replies and new emails with optional file context.
  • Opens Gmail’s composer for your review; it never sends mail automatically.
